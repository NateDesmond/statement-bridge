About this design: The Console

A dense, keyboard-first tool built for someone who imports statements every month and wants zero friction the second time. The warm charcoal base and single amber accent keep long review sessions easy on the eyes without reading as a generic dark-mode template, and status dots plus visible key hints (J/K, X, E, ⌘↵) make the interface feel operable, not just readable. One thing worth a look: the resizer between the PDF and the extracted table on the Review screen actually drags.
