About this design: The Vault

Deep chrome, calm data

Ink-green sidebar and header frame a light, warm-grey workspace, so the app reads like a private back office: the machinery is dark and quiet, the numbers sit on clean paper. A brass accent marks the one action that matters on each screen.

Spreadsheet budgeters are trusting this with bank data. The always-visible offline lock and retention meter say so without a single sentence of legalese, and "Clear sessions" or "Delete everything" get their own deliberate weight instead of hiding in a menu.

Worth noticing: the sidebar meter never moves out of view, no matter which screen is open.
