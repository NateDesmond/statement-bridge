About this design: The Ledger

This option treats the extension like paper accounting: cream ledger stock, hairline rules, a serif for headings and stamped status marks, with every number column set in a tabular monospace and right-aligned like a real cashbook. Red ink means money out, deep green means money in, no other color competes with them.

Spreadsheet budgeters already trust ruled paper and columns of figures more than dashboards. This direction borrows that trust directly: the review table reads like a bank ledger, the PDF source sits inside a page frame with a drop shadow, and confidence badges look stamped rather than gamified.

Worth a look: the balance-check row in Review points straight at the first failing line, and the mapping wizard never auto-confirms a guess, every suggestion has a checkbox-style confirm.
