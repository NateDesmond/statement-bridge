Statement Bridge

Privacy

Last reviewed 18 September 2026.

Statement Bridge reads your bank statements on your own device. There is no account, no server, and nothing about your statements is ever uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • The statement files you drop in
  • The rows extracted from them
  • Any bank layout the extension has learned

What we collect

We collect anonymous, aggregate visits to this website through self-hosted analytics, to see how many people find the site. That analytics has no access to anything inside the extension. The extension itself makes no network requests.
