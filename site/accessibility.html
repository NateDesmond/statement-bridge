Accessibility

We want Statement Bridge and this website to work for everyone, including people using a keyboard, a screen reader, or a browser zoomed in well past normal size.

What we build toward

  • Every control reachable and operable by keyboard alone
  • Clear labels on every button, field, and link
  • Text that stays readable against its background
  • Layouts that hold together when zoomed in or reflowed
  • Motion that respects a reduced-motion setting

This is a direction we hold ourselves to, not a claim that every corner is perfect. A small studio built this, and we keep improving it.
